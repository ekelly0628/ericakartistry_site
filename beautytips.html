Everyday Beauty Tips

A practical prep guide for smoother makeup application, longer wear, and a finish that still looks like skin.

Skin Prep

  • Hydrated skin improves application and longevity.
  • Press products into the skin instead of rubbing.
  • Give skincare a moment to settle before makeup.

Brows

  • Follow your natural brow shape.
  • Use light pressure and short strokes.
  • Focus on sparse areas rather than filling the entire brow.

Foundation

  • Match foundation to the chest and test along the jawline.
  • Use patting motions for a natural finish.
  • Build thin layers instead of applying one heavy layer.

Dimension

  • Contour and bronzer add warmth and shape.
  • Place product where natural shadows occur.
  • Blend upward for a lifted effect.

Concealer

  • Use small amounts to prevent creasing.
  • Place it where light naturally hits the face.
  • Blend outward first, then lightly inward.

Finish

  • Set areas prone to creasing or oil.
  • Keep eyes soft, blended, and wearable.
  • When in doubt, add gloss.
