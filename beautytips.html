Everyday Beauty Tips

Soft · Elevated · Polished

Skin Prep

  • Hydrated skin improves application and longevity
  • Press products into skin, don’t rub

Brows

  • Follow natural brow shape
  • Use light pressure
  • Focus on sparse areas, not entire brow

Foundation

  • Match to chest, test along jawline
  • Use patting motions for natural finish
  • Avoid under eye area to prevent creasing
  • Build in thin layers

Contour / Bronzer

  • Adds warmth and dimension
  • Place where natural shadows occur
  • Blend upward for lifted effect

Blush

  • Adds life and freshness to the face
  • Blend upward softly for seamless transition

Concealer

  • Use small amounts to prevent creasing
  • Place where light hits the face
  • Blend outward, then lightly inward
  • Use patting motions

Powder

  • Set areas prone to creasing/oil
  • Press lightly into skin
  • Avoid over-powdering

Eyes

  • Keep soft, blended, and wearable
  • Blend edges, avoid harsh lines

Lips

  • Lip liner adds structure and definition
  • Blend liner and lip color together
  • When in doubt, add gloss

Key Takeaways

  • Pat and press products into skin
  • Blend thoroughly for seamless finish
  • Thin layers look more natural
  • Makeup should enhance, not mask
